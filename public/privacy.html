SOND3R is a publishing tool and a viewer for files other people publish. There are no accounts, no profiles, and nothing to sign up for, so there is very little about you for us to have. This page says what little there is.

1. What we collect

Nothing beyond the ordinary request logs kept by our infrastructure provider, Cloudflare, which sits in front of this site. Those logs hold what any web server sees: IP address, user agent, the URL requested, and a timestamp. They exist so the service can be kept running and abuse can be stopped, and they are retained under Cloudflare's own policies rather than ours.

We run no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, and no third-party cookies. We do not ask for your name, your email address, or a password. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information, because we do not assemble any.

2. What stays in your browser

Preferences and progress — what you rated, where you stopped watching, a publisher sign-in token — are stored locally by your browser and are not sent to us as a profile. Clearing your browser storage erases them.

3. Wallets and the blockchain

If you connect a wallet, we see its public address and nothing else: never your keys, never your seed phrase. Purchases and publisher registrations settle on a public blockchain, where the transaction, the addresses and the amounts are permanent public records published by the network, not by us. We cannot edit or delete them, and neither can you. Your wallet provider and the payment facilitator are separate services with their own privacy policies.

A publisher's sign-in signature and rights attestation are recorded in our attestation log, which is the compliance record of who claimed rights to what. It holds the wallet address, what was claimed, and when.

4. Content, hosting, and ownership

We do not own, create, or curate the content listed here, and we do not host it in any sense that gives us a copy of it: publishers encrypt their files themselves, we store the ciphertext we cannot read, and public-domain material is served from outside archives we do not run. The catalog is an index. Nothing here is a claim of ownership over any work.

We do not review listings before they appear. Takedown and repeat-infringer handling is in the Terms.

5. Third parties

Using this site necessarily involves parties other than us: Cloudflare, your wallet provider, the payment facilitator, the blockchain and its RPC providers, IPFS gateways, a publisher's own storage, and outside archives. Each handles data under its own terms, which we do not control.

6. Your choices

You can browse without connecting a wallet, and you can clear local storage at any time. Where the law gives you rights over personal data — access, deletion, correction — write to us and we will do what we can, which for on-chain records is nothing, because no one can alter them.

7. Children

The service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always the one at this URL, with its date at the top.
