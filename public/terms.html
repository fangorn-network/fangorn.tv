SOND3R is a publishing tool. Publishers encrypt their own files, set their own prices, and sell directly to buyers. Fangorn LLC operates the software and the relay that stores encrypted files; it is not a party to any sale.

1. What we do not do

These are properties of how the system is built, not just promises:

  • We never hold your money. Payment goes from the buyer's wallet to the publisher's wallet directly on-chain. Fangorn LLC takes no custody of funds at any point and cannot move, freeze, or reverse a payment.
  • We never hold your keys. Every on-chain action is signed by your own wallet. We cannot sign, spend, or publish on your behalf.
  • We cannot read published files. Files are encrypted before storage and the decryption key is released only to a wallet the blockchain says has paid. Neither Fangorn LLC nor any storage provider can decrypt them.

This means we cannot resolve payment disputes, issue refunds, or recover a lost wallet. If you lose access to your wallet, we cannot help you.

3. Takedown

Every published file has an on-chain disable switch. Setting it makes the access gate refuse the decryption key to everyone, including buyers who have already paid. Publishers can use it on their own files at any time from the publisher tools. Fangorn LLC can use it on any file in response to a valid legal notice, a violation of these terms, or a court order.

A takedown does not refund anyone. Payments are final and on-chain; they cannot be reversed by us or by the publisher. A publisher who takes down a file buyers have paid for may owe those buyers a refund directly, and is responsible for arranging it.
